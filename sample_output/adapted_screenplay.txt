TITLE: THE HARVEST DIVIDE (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਮਾਝੀ ਰੂਪਾਂਤਰ / MAJHI DIALECT ADAPTATION)
TARGET REGION: Majha Belt (Amritsar / Tarn Taran Border Tract)
SETTING: Rural Agrarian Homestead
OUTPUT SCRIPT: Gurmukhi & Transliterated Dialogue with Vernacular Annotations

================================================================================
SCENE 1: INT. ANCESTRAL HOMESTEAD - LIVING HALL (BAITHAK) - EARLY MORNING
================================================================================

Early morning rays slant across the baked Nanakshahi brick deori, casting sharp amber geometric shadows on the cowdung-plastered courtyard floor. On the raised brick baithak, an antique brass wall clock swings its pendulum with heavy, steady beats.

BEBE GURMEET KAUR (62), the matriarch, sits upon a low wooden peeri, her gnarled fingers turning the smoothed beads of a wooden Simrana. Around her neck, tied with a greasy black cotton thread, hangs the heavy iron key to the ancestral family sandook.

AMARJIT "AMAR" SINGH (34), the elder brother, stands in the deori threshold in travel-stained clothes: a dark canvas jacket over a faded indigo Khaddar kurta and dusty double-soled desi juttis. He clutches an unsealed manila legal envelope stamped with Tehsil municipal court seals.

AMAR
(hesitant, gripping the envelope)
Sat Sri Akaal, Bebe... Gaddi pichhle pehar pahunch gayi si. Main junction ton taange te aaya aan.

Bebe does not immediately look up. She finishes a full cycle of beads before lifting her piercing gaze.

BEBE
Tinn faslan nikal gayiyan, Amar. Na koi chithhi, na taar. Te ajj tu saade buhe te sarkari kaghaz fadi khada ae... jiven koi tehsil da baaki-vasool karinda hove.

From the inner corridor, ROHANVEER "ROHAN" SINGH (24), the younger brother, enters wiping grease from his work-hardened hands onto a rough cotton towel. He wears a frayed sleeveless vest, rolled-up work trousers, and a tightly tied youthful patka.

ROHAN
(eyes narrowing at Amar)
Eh tuhade gitteyan da dukh puchhan nahi aaya, Bebe! Haath dekho ehde... Patwari di nakal pehlan hi muthhi vich dabbi baitha ae!

AMAR
(stepping forward, voice measured)
Bank da byaj duna ho gaya si, Rohan! Je ajj dopahar ton pehlan intiqal te daskhat na hoye, taan district cooperative court ne thall-le chaar kille kurki kar dene ne!

ROHAN
(stepping between Amar and Bebe)
Oh chaar kille saanu saari sardi rozi dinde rahe jad tu shehar vich thekedari karda si! Tu taange ton utar ke saade Bapu de daure de battey nahi wand sakda!

Bebe taps her heavy wooden lathi on the stone threshold once. Absolute silence descends.

BEBE
Khaali pet koi bahi te daskhat nahi karda. Lifafa aale te rakh, Amar. Te aapan canal di motor te Patwari nu milde aan.


================================================================================
SCENE 2: EXT. FAMILY ORCHARD - CANAL TUBE-WELL (MOTOR) - AFTERNOON
================================================================================

Dupahar di tatti loh kinnowan de baghan te agg vargi chal rahi ae. Canal de kandhe te Kirloskar da diesel engine 'thuk-thuk-thuk' karda khalse vich paani sutt riha ae.

Amar arrives along the dirt embankment. He has discarded his canvas jacket, wearing only his dust-streaked Khaddar kurta, but the manila legal envelope is tucked firmly under his left arm.

PATWARI GURDIP SINGH (52), the municipal land revenue officer, stands beside the canal bridge under an umbrella. He wears a khaki safari suit, thick horn-rimmed glasses, and holds a heavy brass-jointed measuring chain (zarib) and a cloth-wrapped land register.

GURDIP
(wiping sweat with a handkerchief)
Aao ji, shehri Sardar ji! Tuhada chhota veer taan aakhda si ki Patwari di zarib nu canal di motor ch fansa dena ae.

Amar approaches, extending the legal envelope.

AMAR
Boln de ehnu, Patwari ji. Eh dekho baasi assi di intiqal nakal! Uttri khalsa saade hisse da hakk banta ae.

Gurdip takes the envelope, pulling out the yellowed 1982 blueprint.

GURDIP
(squinting at the blueprint)
Kaghazan te taan theek ae, Sardar ji. Par tuhade Bapu ne das saal pehlan pind di water committee naal zubani vaada kitta si. Je tussi eh khalsa tod ditta, taan pichhle tin kisaan pani ton aat-te ho jaangey.

Rohan marches up the embankment carrying a heavy rusted iron pipe spanner, his vest drenched in sweat from repairing the intake.

ROHAN
Daso ehnu, Gurdip ji! Bapu di beemari aali sardi da vaada daso! Ehnu lagda ae ki zameen nile pen di siyahi naal banndi ae!

Amar steps close to Rohan, confronting him chest to chest.

AMAR
Main har maheene dak-khane ton paise bheje si! Har ik tankha da hissa post-order vich paaya si!

ROHAN
Kaagzan de note pohh di raat nu canal di gaadh nahi kadd de, Bhau! Jiss din tu lohe di peti binn ke shahar gaya si, osse din is paani te tera hakk muk gaya si!


================================================================================
SCENE 3: INT. HOMESTEAD COURTYARD (VEHRA) AND KITCHEN - DUSK
================================================================================

Dusk settles in shades of deep indigo over the baked brick walls. Swallows dart across the open sky of the courtyard. In the sheltered veranda hearth (chulha), Bebe stirs a simmering brass patila of warm spiced milk and turmeric.

Amar sits on a low wooden manja in the corner. The opened legal envelope lies beside him, its red ribbon loose on the wooden planks.

Bebe pours two steaming Kansa bowls and carries them over with steady, deliberate steps. She hands one bowl to Amar.

BEBE
Tuhade Bapu di khabbi ragg vi aidan hi fadakdi si jadon bank di chithhi aandi si.

Amar takes the warm bowl, his hands trembling visibly.

AMAR
(voice dropping, cracking)
Mandi di aadat dubb gayi, Bebe... Chhe maheene pehlan sab ujad gaya. Main bas sharam de maare bol na sakeya... warehouse de thaddiyan te so reha si.

Bebe watches him quietly, neither flinching nor scolding.

BEBE
Saanu pata si.

Amar looks up, startled.

AMAR
Kiven?

BEBE
Maa nu pata lagg jaanda ae jadon chithhiyan vich ghare da haal puchhan di thaan kismat diyan safayiyan aavan lagg pain. Par zameen koi bahi-khaata nahi hundi, Amar. Tu Rohan da paseena bech ke aapna dukh nahi dho sakda.

The iron latch of the main deori gate rattles. Rohan enters, carrying a hurricane lantern and an old brass kerosene can. His anger has simmered into brooding exhaustion.


================================================================================
SCENE 4: EXT. ANCESTRAL BOUNDARY BANYAN TREE (BOHARH) - NIGHT
================================================================================

Puranmashi da chann wadde purane boharh diyan jhadan vichon chandi vargi chhanw sutt riha ae, jithe dade-parde de zameen di burji gaddi hoyi ae. Door canal di jhalar te tiddon di aawaz aundi ae.

Rohan stands near the whitewashed stone marker, planting his lantern on a flat boulder.

Amar approaches through the tall canal grass, holding the heavy brass chest key that Bebe gave him before leaving the courtyard.

ROHAN
(without turning around)
The assessor offered seventy thousand for the road frontage. Oil mill aale truck langhaun layi mang rahe ne.

Amar stops beside the ancient banyan trunk. He looks at the key in his palm, then at the moonlit trees stretching toward the canal.

AMAR
Main Patwari nu aakh ditta... farad faad ke chulhe ch jhonk de! Kaghaz sandook vich hi rahangey. Zameen da paani nahi wandeya jaana.

Rohan turns slowly, disbelief flickering across his shadowed face.

ROHAN
Tu ki kitta?

AMAR
(stepping to the stone marker)
Main do haath te tin saal da karza utaran layi wapis aaya aan. Je tu savere naal khet ch khada karein.

Rohan looks at Amar's outstretched hand. He looks at the crumpled legal papers sticking out of Amar's back pocket, discarded and rendered powerless.

Slowly, Rohan clasps Amar's forearm with a fierce, unyielding Punjabi grip.

ROHAN
Tadke chaar vaje motor challu karni ae, Bhau. Te filter pehlan tu saaf karenga.

FADE OUT.
