# KathaSetu Costume Bible: The Harvest Divide
**Target Culture:** Majhi (Punjab, Majha Belt)

---

## 1. COSTUME PLATE `COST_AMAR_01`: Amar Travel Attire
* **Worn By:** `CHAR_AMAR` (Amarjit Singh)
* **Scenes Appearing:** `SC01` (Living Hall Arrival), `SC02` (Canal Well Confrontation)
* **Garment Breakdown:**
  - **Upper:** Dark heavy cotton canvas utility jacket over an indigo blue handspun Khaddar collared Kurta.
  - **Lower:** Heavy charcoal twill field trousers with reinforced knees.
  - **Headwear:** Crisp, starch-ironed navy blue Sikh Pagri with traditional Shamla.
  - **Footwear:** Double-leather soled Desi Juttis, softened with mustard oil, road-dusted with yellow canal silt.
  - **Accessories:** Solid iron Sarbloh Kara on right wrist.
* **Textile & Dye Specs:** Handspun coarse Khaddar cotton (180 GSM), natural indigo dye with wash fading at collar and cuffs.

## 2. COSTUME PLATE `COST_AMAR_02`: Amar Homestead Domestic Attire
* **Worn By:** `CHAR_AMAR` (Amarjit Singh)
* **Scenes Appearing:** `SC03` (Courtyard Confession), `SC04` (Midnight Boundary Reconciliation)
* **Narrative Change Justification:**
  Amar has washed off travel grime with well water after the canal confrontation. Discarded the rigid city canvas jacket to sit with his mother and face his brother.
* **Garment Breakdown:**
  - **Upper:** Soft unbleached off-white organic cotton Kurta with mother-of-pearl buttons.
  - **Lower:** Loose cotton Chadra / relaxed Pajama in matching ivory weave.
  - **Headwear:** Soft home-tied saffron cloth turban without rigid starched pleats.
  - **Footwear:** Simple slip-on leather mojris or barefoot inside courtyard veranda.

## 3. COSTUME PLATE `COST_ROHAN_01`: Rohan Agrarian Work Attire
* **Worn By:** `CHAR_ROHAN` (Rohanveer Singh)
* **Scenes Appearing:** `SC01`, `SC02`, `SC03`, `SC04` (Unchanged through single grueling harvest day)
* **Garment Breakdown:**
  - **Upper:** Coarse olive-green sleeveless cotton banyan/vest with fraying armholes, sweat stains at chest.
  - **Lower:** Rugged denim work trousers rolled up to mid-calf to prevent dragging in canal mud.
  - **Headwear:** Tightly knotted black Patka turban keeping long hair contained during engine maintenance.
  - **Footwear:** Heavy rubber-and-leather agrarian field boots caked in dry irrigation silt.
  - **Props in hand:** Rusted Kirloskar diesel pump iron spanner, rough cotton grease towel.

## 4. COSTUME PLATE `COST_BEBE_01`: Bebe Matriarchal Garb
* **Worn By:** `CHAR_BEBE` (Bebe Gurmeet Kaur)
* **Scenes Appearing:** `SC01`, `SC03` (Ancestral Homestead compound)
* **Garment Breakdown:**
  - **Main Garment:** Classic ivory unbleached cotton Salwar-Kameez tailored with generous room for mobility.
  - **Over-Drape:** Museum-grade heirloom Phulkari shawl in rich mustard-gold and madder-red geometric diamond silk floss embroidery (Pat silk thread).
  - **Headwear:** Phulkari respectfully drawn over head, framing silver hair.
  - **Footwear:** Flat dark velvet juttis lined with soft wool for arthritic relief.
  - **Key Jewelry:** Heavy antique iron chest key tied with a thick black braided cotton thread resting at heart level; silver hoop earrings (Kantian).

## 5. COSTUME PLATE `COST_GURDIP_01`: Patwari Government Uniform
* **Worn By:** `CHAR_GURDIP` (Patwari Gurdip Singh)
* **Scenes Appearing:** `SC02` (Canal Tube-Well)
* **Garment Breakdown:**
  - **Main Garment:** Khaki fawn terylene safari suit with two buttoned chest pockets holding blue and red fountain pens and a cloth measuring tape.
  - **Footwear:** Dusted brown Bata oxford shoes.

  - **Accessories:** Black folding umbrella, brass-linked land measuring chain (zarib).
